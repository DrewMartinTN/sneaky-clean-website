Case Notes

Quick answers.

Can I book just an interior clean?

Yes. Tap Book Interior Only to open our booking form with Interior Only selected. Choose your vehicle size and request an available appointment. Refresh and Full Reset include interior and exterior work.

Will every stain or odor come out?

Results depend on the material, the source and how long it has been there. Send clear photos and describe any odors so we can explain realistic expectations and recommend the right work before you decide.

How much does Interior Detail cost?

Base prices before tax are $150 for sedans, $180 for small SUVs and small trucks, and $210 for large SUVs, 3-row vehicles and full-size trucks. Pet hair removal, extraction, odor treatment and excessive trash removal are additional services that we review with you before approval.

Can you handle family vehicle messes?

Yes. Tell us about the mess and send photos of the areas that need attention so we can quote the work your interior needs.

Do you need photos before interior detailing?

Photos are optional. Clear interior photos of stains, pet hair, debris and problem areas help us confirm the scope. Any additional work or charges require your approval.

How long does an interior detail take?

The base detail reserves about 2 hours for sedans, 2.5 hours for small SUVs and small trucks, and 3 hours for large SUVs, 3-row vehicles and full-size trucks. Selected add-ons can add time.

Do you come to my location?

Yes. Sneaky Clean is mobile and serves Murfreesboro and surrounding Middle Tennessee areas.
