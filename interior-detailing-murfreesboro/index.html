Case Notes

Quick answers.

Can I book just an interior clean?

Yes. Interior-only detailing is quoted separately after we review your vehicle and its condition. You do not need to purchase an exterior detail. Our Refresh and Full Reset online packages include both interior and exterior work.

Will every stain or odor come out?

Results depend on the material, the source and how long it has been there. Send clear photos and describe any odors so we can explain realistic expectations and recommend the right work before you decide.

How much does Interior Detail cost?

Interior Detail has variable pricing. The price depends on the interior condition and the work required, so we provide a custom quote after reviewing your vehicle or photos.

Can you handle family vehicle messes?

Yes. Tell us about the mess and send photos of the areas that need attention so we can quote the work your interior needs.

Do you need photos before interior detailing?

Please send interior photos, especially of stains, pet hair, debris and problem areas, along with your vehicle year, make and model. We use these details to prepare your quote.

How long does an interior detail take?

Timing depends on the condition of the interior and the cleaning required. We will discuss the expected time when preparing your quote.

Do you come to my location?

Yes. Sneaky Clean is mobile and serves Murfreesboro and surrounding Middle Tennessee areas.
