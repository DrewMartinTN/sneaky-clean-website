Detailing at your home, office or apartment

You do not need to drop off the vehicle. Tell us where it will be parked and confirm that the property allows mobile detailing. Apartments, shared parking and office lots need permission and enough room to work safely around the vehicle. Call before booking if you are unsure about your location.

What should I tell you before the appointment?

Include your vehicle year, make and model, the service address and the areas that need attention. Mention pet hair, stains or odors so the work can be scoped accurately. Photos are useful for problem areas. Photos are optional for Interior Only booking, and any additional work needs your approval.

Case Notes

Quick answers.

Do you come to homes and offices in Murfreesboro?

Yes. Sneaky Clean is mobile and serves homes, offices, apartments, and approved locations when the setup allows.

What mobile detail should I book?

Book a Refresh Detail for maintained vehicles. Choose a Full Reset Detail for daily drivers, family vehicles, and interiors that need deeper attention.

How long does mobile detailing take?

Refresh Details take about 2 to 2.5 hours. Full Reset Details take about 4 to 5 hours, depending on vehicle size.

Can I call before booking?

Yes. Calling is often the fastest way to choose the right package or decide if photos are needed for a quote.

Can I book interior-only car cleaning in Murfreesboro?

Yes. Tap Book Interior Only, choose your vehicle size, and request an available appointment. Base prices before tax are $150 for sedans, $180 for small SUVs and small trucks, and $210 for large SUVs, 3-row vehicles and full-size trucks. Describe pet hair, stains or odors in your booking notes so any extra work can be reviewed with you before approval.
