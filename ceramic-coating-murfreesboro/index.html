Case Notes

Quick answers.

How much does ceramic coating cost?

Ceramic coating is quoted after review because paint condition, vehicle size, and prep needs can change the price.

Do I need paint correction first?

Sometimes. If swirls or dullness will be locked under the coating, paint correction may be recommended before coating.

Do coated vehicles still need maintenance?

Yes. Coatings make maintenance easier, but they still need proper washing and periodic check-ins.
