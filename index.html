Do you come to me?

Yes. Sneaky Clean is a mobile detailing company. We bring the setup to your home, office, or approved location.

How much does mobile detailing cost?

Refresh Details start at $199. Full Reset Details start at $299. Paint correction, ceramic coating, and motorcycles are quoted after review.

What areas do you serve?

We regularly serve Murfreesboro, Smyrna, Nashville, La Vergne, and surrounding Middle Tennessee communities.

Yes. We offer maintenance details and check-ins to help coated vehicles stay cleaner and easier to wash.

What days can I book online?

Online booking with live times runs Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday. Need another day? Text 615-481-0464 and we'll try to fit a rush slot.

Do you offer a monthly plan?

Yes. The monthly plan is $150/month for one detail every month at your location, and you can alternate between two vehicles.
