Information we collect

  • Contact and appointment information: your name, phone number, email if provided, service address or ZIP code, preferred contact method, selected services, appointment times and messages.
  • Vehicle and service information: vehicle year, make, model, size, condition, requested extras, notes, photos you provide, service photos and work records. Images may incidentally contain a plate, location or personal belongings; please avoid including people, documents or unrelated personal information.
  • Transaction information: quotes, approvals, invoice and payment status, deposit records, transaction identifiers and receipts. Square processes card details through its payment tools; we do not collect full card numbers through our ordinary booking forms.
  • Website information: pages visited, interactions such as booking starts and call-button clicks, device and browser information, IP address and approximate location, referral information and campaign labels. Some of this is collected by hosting and analytics providers.
  • Consent and communications: your communication preferences, relevant consent records, opt-out requests and customer-support history.

How we use information

We use information to answer inquiries, prepare and review quotes, schedule and perform detailing, communicate about your request, take authorized payments, manage memberships, keep business records, address service concerns, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. We also use website activity and referral information to understand which pages and marketing channels lead to inquiries and bookings.

Optional vehicle photos may be reviewed by our team and, when automated photo review is enabled, sent to OpenAI to help assess visible condition and recommend a service. The team can review the result, and extra work or price changes require your approval. You can contact us for a team review instead. Submitting quote photos does not authorize their use in advertising; we ask permission before using your submitted photos for promotion.

Who receives information

We share information as needed with service providers that help operate the business:

  • Square: customer records, appointments, payment processing, invoices, memberships and related notifications.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub: website hosting, booking infrastructure, storage, security and technical request handling.
  • Google services: business email, private vehicle-photo storage in Google Drive, and website analytics and advertising measurement.
  • OpenAI: vehicle images and relevant vehicle or condition details when automated photo review is enabled.
  • Email and messaging providers, including Resend: delivering request alerts, appointment communications and support messages.

Authorized staff and people performing your service may access the details needed for their work. Providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy notices. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect people, investigate fraud, resolve disputes or protect legal rights. If the business is transferred, relevant records may transfer subject to applicable law and this policy.

We do not sell or rent customer contact lists. The specific restrictions on mobile information and messaging consent below also apply.

Mobile information and text messages

We use your mobile number and messaging preferences to communicate about services you request and send other messages only with the required consent. Marketing consent is separate from appointment communications and is not a condition of purchase.

We do not share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in records or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. This restriction applies even where another section of this policy discusses sharing. Providers that deliver messages or support the service may process that information as necessary to perform those services.

Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. You can also email sneakycleantn@gmail.com or call (717) 870-9439. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. We may keep limited consent and opt-out records to document your preferences and avoid contacting you against your wishes. See our text messaging terms.

Cookies, local storage and Google measurement

Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Ads tags to measure visits, interactions and advertising results. These tools can use cookies or similar technologies and receive device, browser, IP address, page and interaction information. Their data practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Our analytics event code does not intentionally send the customer name, phone number, email, service address, uploaded photos or private estimate links as event fields.

We also use browser local storage to remember first and recent referral sources, public landing-page paths and campaign labels. These attribution records are treated as expired by our code after 90 days from the first recorded visit; browser data can remain until overwritten or cleared. Local storage may also help avoid counting the same booking event repeatedly. Attribution information can be attached to your booking and customer record.

You can control cookies and clear local storage through your browser, use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and manage eligible ad personalization through Google My Ad Center. Blocking storage or scripts can affect website features and measurement. Our site does not currently change its tracking behavior in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. You can request help or discuss privacy choices with us by phone or email.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to fulfill the purposes described here, maintain service and transaction records, meet tax or other legal obligations, resolve disputes and honor communication preferences. Retention varies by record type and provider; this policy does not promise automatic deletion of every business record after a fixed period.

We use access restrictions and other reasonable safeguards for business records and private photos. No website, storage system or message transmission is completely secure. Providers may process or store information in the United States or other locations where they operate. Please do not send payment-card numbers or unrelated sensitive information in form notes, email or text messages.

Your choices and requests

Contact sneakycleantn@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, or to ask about our data practices. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond as required by applicable law; some records may need to be retained for completed transactions, legal obligations, security or opt-out preferences. You may authorize someone to make a request where applicable law permits.

You can choose not to provide optional information or photos, and can arrange service by phone or email if you prefer. Information necessary to provide a quote, contact you or perform service may still be required. Tell us if your preferred contact method changes. Promotional email, if you separately subscribe, includes an unsubscribe option.

Children and outside links

Our services are intended for adults arranging vehicle care, and our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can review and address it.

Links to Google, Square, social networks and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Their practices are outside this policy.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our services or tools change. The effective date identifies the current version. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before materially changing how previously collected information is used.
